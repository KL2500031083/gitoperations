a letter from your little girl

Happy Birthday, Amma ♥

To the woman who made me believe in myself before I even knew how to.

Happy birthday to the most beautiful person in my world.

I love you more than I could ever put into words. You are not just my mother; you are my best friend, my biggest support, and the person who has made me feel loved, understood, and free to be myself.

When I look at my life, I realise that so much of who I am today has been shaped by you.

You have always wanted to be a part of my life, not just as my mother, but as someone who would experience things with me. You even gave up your job because you wanted to spend more time with me. At that age, I may not have understood what that meant, but today I realise how much of yourself you gave just to be there for me.

You have always made me feel special.

One of the memories that means so much to me is how my journey in sports began.

You knew how much I loved swimming, and because you saw that happiness in me, you encouraged me to join swimming. But it wasn't easy. People started questioning your decision.

They would say things like, “She is your only daughter. Why are you sending her into sports? You are spoiling her. She won't concentrate on her studies. She is wasting her time.”

People had their own opinions about what I should be doing with my life.

But you didn't let their opinions decide my future.

“You trusted me.♥

You trusted that my interest in swimming meant something. You trusted that I deserved the chance to explore what I loved.

And what makes that memory even more special is that you didn't just put me into swimming and leave me there.

“You learnt swimming along with me.♥

You wanted to spend more and more time with me. You wanted to be there with me, experience those moments with me, and be a part of something that I loved.

You didn't simply watch my journey from the side.

“You stepped into it with me.♥

That is something I will always carry in my heart.

You supported me through swimming, rowing, and everything that followed. Even when people told you that sports were a waste of time and that I should only focus on studies, you continued to believe in me.

Whenever I went through difficult or low moments, you were there to lift me back up. You motivated me when I doubted myself and reminded me to keep going when things became difficult.

You never made me feel that my dreams were foolish.

Instead, you gave me the courage to pursue them.

Because of that belief, my journey took me much further than I could have imagined.

I went from simply loving sports to competing, representing, travelling, learning from every experience, and eventually reaching 11 Nationals.

When I look at that achievement today, I don't see only the competitions or the numbers.

I see everything that came before them.

I see the mother who believed in me when people questioned me.

I see the person who stood beside me when others thought I was wasting my time.

I see you.

And that is why every achievement of mine will always have a part of you in it.

But sports are only one part of what you have given me.

You have also given me something that I now realise is incredibly rare — the freedom to choose my own life.

I have seen this closely through my own friends and their families. I have seen some of my friends being expected to study what their parents wanted them to study and choose careers based on their parents' interests or expectations.

Many of them were not given enough space to stop and ask themselves what they genuinely wanted.

They weren't always given the freedom to think about their own interests, discover what they were passionate about, and choose a career for themselves.

And whenever I see that, I realise even more how lucky I am to have you.

Because you never made my future about your expectations.

You gave me the freedom to think.

You gave me the freedom to explore.

You gave me the freedom to make my own decisions.

Most importantly, you respected those decisions.

You never told me, “This is what you have to become.”

Instead, you made me feel, “Think about what makes you happy, and choose it.”

That freedom has shaped me in ways I may never be able to fully explain.

You taught me that my life belongs to me, while always being there to guide me whenever I needed you.

You didn't simply decide what I should become.

“You gave me the chance to decide who I wanted to become.♥

And Amma, that is one of the greatest gifts you have ever given me.

When I think about everything you have done, I realise that your love was never only in words.

It was in the choices you made.

It was in the time you gave me.

It was in the sacrifices you made.

It was in standing beside me when other people questioned me.

It was in learning swimming just so you could share that experience with me.

It was in supporting my sports journey even when people told you it was a waste of time.

It was in giving me the freedom to choose my own career.

And it was in being there during the moments when I needed you the most.

I may not always say all of this to you.

I may not always know how to express how much I appreciate you.

But I remember.

I notice.

And as I grow older, I understand more and more of the things you did for me that I couldn't fully understand when I was younger.

If I have the courage to dream today, a huge part of that courage comes from you.

If I have the confidence to make my own choices, it is because you trusted me.

If I have achieved things that once felt impossible, it is because you gave me the freedom to try.

And if I am able to look at my future with hope, it is because I have always known that I have you behind me.

“Without you, I am nothing today.♥

Every achievement I have is connected to a journey that you helped me build.

Every dream I chase carries a little bit of the faith you placed in me.

You didn't just raise me.

You gave me the confidence to become myself.

You didn't just support my dreams.

You gave me the space to discover them.

And you didn't just stand beside me when things were easy.

You stayed when things were difficult too.

I don't know what the future will bring or where life will take me.

But I know that wherever I go and whatever I become, I will always remember the person who gave me the courage to dream freely.

You will always be that person for me.

Amma, I wish I could somehow show you the world through my eyes for just one day.

Maybe then you would understand how much you mean to me.

You are the person I can come back to.

The person whose presence makes everything feel a little easier.

The person who has seen me at my happiest and at my lowest, and loved me through all of it.

You are my home in a person.

And no matter how much I grow, how many places I travel to, how many achievements I add to my life, or how independent I become...

“I will always be your little girl.♥

I hope one day I can give you even a fraction of the happiness, freedom, and love that you have given me.

Until then, I just want you to know that I carry your love with me in everything I do.

Happy Birthday, Amma. ♥

To the woman who believed in me before I knew how to believe in myself.

To the woman who chose to walk beside me instead of choosing my path for me.

To the woman who made me feel that my dreams were worth chasing.

I love you more than words can ever explain.

Forever and always,
Your little girl. ♥
